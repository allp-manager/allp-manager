Allp 0.6.5 is an opt-in prerelease. Download the archive for your platform below, or use `allp self-update --update-channel prerelease` from a compatible standalone install.

- Keep Pacman upgrades as full sync-and-upgrade operations and show accurate APT, Flatpak, and Snap results.
- List and remove Node and Python tools with their original installer. Profiles retain the installer and the scope of Flatpak applications.
- Keep a copy of the previous executable after self-update and restore it if verification fails.
- Fix macOS user lookup and validate the Linux ARM64, Windows, and macOS paths with native test jobs. CI retains per-platform test logs.

The archives include SHA-256 checksums and a manifest for the prerelease channel.
